Smart City Intelligence Hub

A free, interactive platform that maps the smart city and IoT ecosystem as one connected knowledge graph.

Open the live platform ↗
Next.jsReactTypeScriptTailwind CSSshadcn/uiReact Flow

The problem

Information in smart cities and IoT is fragmented. MQTT is explained in one place, digital twins in another and smart buildings somewhere else, so it is hard to see how it all connects.

Who it is for

People entering or advancing in smart cities, IoT and product management.

My role

Product owner, designer and builder.

Approach

  • 22+ IoT protocols explained from beginner to technical depth.
  • Smart city subsystems and how they interact.
  • 18+ solution architectures with KPIs and implementation notes.
  • Global case studies including Singapore, Dubai, Barcelona, NEOM, Amsterdam, Seoul and Masdar City.
  • An interactive knowledge graph so users explore connections, not isolated definitions.

Results

  • Started as a personal learning tool. I now use it for research, client conversations and solution design.

What is next

  • Gather feedback from practitioners
  • Decide what to add next, based on what users ask for
