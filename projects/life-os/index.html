LIFE OS (Project 2035)

A local first planning app that connects long term goals to what to do today, and adapts when you fall behind.

Open the live demo ↗
Next.jsTypeScriptSQLiteAutomated tests

The problem

Goals fall apart the first time you miss a week. Goals lived in notes, numbers lived in my head, and daily tasks had no link to either.

Who it is for

Someone in a transition, rebuilding routines, money or a career, who wants structure without guilt and does not want to hand personal data to a cloud service.

My role

Product owner, designer and builder.

Approach

  • Every long term goal is cut into milestones and small daily actions.
  • Plans adapt to capacity with Minimum, Standard and Deep Work modes.
  • A daily plan that adapts to capacity, regular check ins on energy, mood, wins and blockers, a six stage roadmap, money tracking and a growth area for habits and learning.

Product decisions

  • Staged, not parallel. Six ordered life stages, one in focus at a time, because nine areas competing for attention is how people quit.
  • A Minimum Day. Every routine has a smaller version that still counts, because the product is judged on whether it survives a bad week.
  • Plans are seeded, facts are not. No balance, application or achievement is pre filled, so every number on screen was entered by the user.
  • Scope is a feature. A broad brief became eight modules. The rest is sequenced, not dropped.

Results

  • Works as a personal tool, with a public demo on fictional data.

What is next

  • Keep using it daily and fix what breaks
  • Decide whether it is useful to anyone but me
