Personal Experiments and Product Discovery

Treating my own life as a product

Personal project, 2026.

The problem

Vision boards and long roadmaps feel productive but do not tell you what to do at 9 a.m. on a Tuesday. My goals lived in notes, my numbers lived in my head, and my daily tasks had no link to either.

What I did

  • Wrote the problem, user and approach down before building, as I would for any product.
  • Scoped a broad brief into an eight module MVP and sequenced the rest.
  • Built it local first so personal data stays on my device.

Decisions and trade offs

  • One life stage in focus at a time, with later stages locked until the current one is stable.
  • A smaller Minimum Day that still counts.
  • Actuals and projections are never mixed, and no number is pre filled.

Demonstrated

  • A working app with a demo mode on fictional data.

Not claimed

  • Hypothesis: that visibility beats motivation. I am still testing it on myself, with a sample of one.

What I took from it

You cannot solve what you cannot see. Saying not yet matters as much as saying yes.
